# Salão Juliana Moura - Design System

## Color Strategy: Committed (Refined)
- **Base**: OKLCH(99% 0.003 340) - Ultra-light warm white.
- **Surface**: OKLCH(97% 0.01 340) - Subtle rose-tinted background.
- **Accent (Dusty Rose)**: OKLCH(70% 0.08 340) - Sophisticated, muted boutique rose.
- **Text**: OKLCH(20% 0.01 340) - Deep, warm charcoal.

## Typography
- **Logo/Branding**: Libre Franklin (Black weight) - Bold, modern, authoritative.
- **Headings**: Playfair Display (Serif) - Luxury and elegance.
- **Body/UI**: Geist Sans - Clean, modern, high performance.

## Layout & Spacing
- **Gutter**: 2rem (32px) on mobile, up to 5rem (80px) on desktop.
- **Grid**: Fluid single column on mobile, offset two-column or balanced grids on desktop.
- **Depth**: Soft shadows (shadow-xl) and subtle background blurs.

## Motion
- **Curves**: Ease-out-expo for all entries.
- **Threshold**: Viewport-triggered reveals for all major sections.
- **Interactions**: Subtle scale up (1.02) on hover for cards.
