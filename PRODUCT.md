# Salão Juliana Moura - Product Strategy

## Purpose
A high-end digital presence for a luxury hair and nail salon in São Paulo. The goal is to showcase services (hair, blowout, manicure, pedicure) and convert visitors into appointments.

## Users
- Primary: Women in São Paulo looking for professional, expert beauty services.
- Value: Reliability, hygiene (biosafety), technical expertise, and a welcoming environment.

## Brand Voice
- **Tone**: Elegant, professional, expert, but warm and accessible.
- **Vibe**: High-end boutique salon. Not a commercial chain. Personal touch from Juliana Moura.

## Strategic Principles
- **Experience (E-E-A-T)**: Highlight Juliana's 10+ years of expertise.
- **Biosafety**: Emphasize sterilization and hygiene as a luxury standard.
- **Clarity**: Easy access to prices, services, and scheduling.
- **Performance**: Instant loading, fluid animations, flawless mobile experience.
